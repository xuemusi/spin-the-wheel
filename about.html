About SpinTheWheel.buzz

Welcome to SpinTheWheel.buzz, the premier online cryptographic random decision studio designed for classrooms, live streams, game nights, and everyday choices.

Our Core Philosophy: 100% Fairness & Privacy

Traditional spinning wheels often use weak pseudo-random algorithms or clunky flash animations. We built SpinTheWheel.buzz from the ground up using modern Web Crypto APIs (crypto.getRandomValues), guaranteeing mathematical equality for every slice on the wheel.

We respect your privacy: your lists, student names, and custom wheels stay 100% in your private local browser storage. We never collect or store your inputs on remote servers.

Whiteboard & Stream Ready

Whether you are a teacher engaging a room full of students on an interactive smartboard, a Twitch/YouTube streamer hosting giveaways, or friends deciding on dinner, our tool is crafted to be lightning fast and zero friction.
