About Spin the Wheel

Spin the Wheel is a free online random picker tool. Enter your options, spin a colorful wheel, and get a random result instantly.

Why We Built It

We wanted a random picker that is fast, beautiful, and free. No popups, no signup walls, no clutter. Just type your options and spin.

What Makes It Different

  • No signup or account required — ever.
  • Works on any device: phone, tablet, desktop.
  • Real randomness using your browser's crypto random number generator.
  • Your data stays in your browser — nothing is sent to a server.
  • Multiple wheel modes: custom, names, yes/no, prize.

Privacy First

Spin the Wheel does not collect, store, or transmit your data. Your option lists are saved only in your browser's local storage. We do not use cookies to track you. See our Privacy Policy for details.
