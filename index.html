Frequently Asked Questions

Is the spin the wheel really random?

Yes. We use your browser's built-in crypto random number generator to ensure every option has an equal probability of being selected.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Spin the Wheel is completely free to use with no signup required. Just visit the page and start spinning.

How many options can I add?

You can add up to 100 options per wheel in the free version. Each option appears as a colored section on the wheel.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The wheel is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops with touch and click support.

Can I share my wheel results?

Yes. After spinning, you can take a screenshot of the result to share with your class, team, or friends.
