Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Summary

Spin the Wheel is a client-side web application. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data. All data remains in your browser.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require accounts or logins.
  • We do not collect names, emails, or any personal information.
  • We do not use tracking cookies.
  • We do not store your wheel options or results on any server.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties.

Local Storage

Your wheel options are saved in your browser's local storage so you can return and find your list. This data never leaves your device. To clear it, use your browser's "Clear browsing data" feature or click the Clear button on the wheel.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to count page views and understand which features are used. These analytics do not track individuals or store personal data.

Advertising

This site may display ads from third-party networks. These networks may use cookies as described in their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing the ad network's privacy policy for details.

Children's Privacy

Spin the Wheel is suitable for classroom use and does not knowingly collect any data from children or anyone else.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, please review our Terms of Service for contact information.
