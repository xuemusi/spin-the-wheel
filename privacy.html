Privacy Policy

At SpinTheWheel.buzz, we value and respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to zero tracking and client-side data safety.

1. Client-Side Data Storage

All choice lists, student rosters, names, and customized wheel settings are saved strictly inside your device's browser localStorage. We do not transmit or store your inputs on any backend server.

2. Zero Cookies & Zero Personal Profiling

We do not use invasive tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or user profiling tools. The application runs purely in your browser.

3. Cryptographic Fairness

Wheel outcomes are generated using the Web Cryptography API (crypto.getRandomValues) directly inside your browser engine.
