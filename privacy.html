Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026.

At SpinTheWheel.buzz, accessible from https://spinthewheel.buzz, your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information we do and do not collect.

1. Client-Side Data Processing

All wheel options, custom lists, and winning histories you enter into SpinTheWheel.buzz are stored strictly in your device's browser localStorage. This data never touches our servers.

2. Server Logs & Cookies

Like standard web hosts, our server infrastructure (Cloudflare) logs basic request data such as IP address and browser user-agent for DDoS mitigation and performance delivery. We do not sell or monetize personal user data.

3. Contact

If you have any questions or feedback regarding our privacy practices, feel free to contact us through our website.
