Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use this for a company raffle?

Yes. Enter all employee names, project the wheel on a big screen, and spin to select winners. The process is visible to everyone.

Can I pick multiple winners?

Yes. After each spin, remove the winner from the wheel and spin again. This ensures no one wins twice.
