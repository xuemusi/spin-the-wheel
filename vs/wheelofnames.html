1. Cryptographic Randomness vs Standard Math.random()

When conducting a high-stakes classroom lottery, prize giveaway, or team assignment, true mathematical fairness is paramount. Standard JavaScript Math.random() relies on pseudorandom number generators (PRNG) like Xoroshiro128+, which can exhibit periodic clustering patterns when executed across rapid successive iterations.

SpinTheWheel.buzz integrates the browser's hardware entropy pool via window.crypto.getRandomValues(new Uint32Array(1)). This guarantees non-deterministic, cryptographically secure randomness (CSPRNG) with uniform probability across every single wedge, regardless of wheel size.

2. Student Data Privacy (COPPA & FERPA Compliance)

Teachers frequently paste sensitive student rosters, full names, and group pairings into online random pickers. Under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), transmitting student personally identifiable information (PII) to unverified third-party servers poses serious compliance risks.

SpinTheWheel operates strictly on a zero-retention client-side model. Your lists are stored only in your local browser's localStorage. No names are sent across any network socket, ensuring full compliance for K-12 schools, universities, and enterprise HR departments.
