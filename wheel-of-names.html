Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pick multiple names?

Yes. After each spin, the selected name is removed from the wheel. Spin again to pick the next person. This ensures no one is picked twice.

Can I save my class list?

Your name list is automatically saved in your browser. When you return, your list will be there. Clear your browser data to reset.

How many names can I add?

Up to 100 names in the free version. For most classrooms and raffles, this is more than enough.
